---
name: Frozen assessment safeguards
description: How to handle qualitative construct requirements in the frozen Entrepreneur DNA instrument
---

The frozen specification defines identity-specific construct safeguards but does not provide a per-option construct-tag table. Treat the interpretation of qualifying answers as an explicit, reviewable layer separate from the frozen question wording and numeric weights. For Dual, interpret meaningful multi-facet evidence as direct identity support from at least two distinct facets, in addition to the three-facet breadth requirement for each identity.

**Why:** Inferring a construct from incidental cross-identity points can grant an identity without evidence of its defining behavior; changing the instrument's weights to compensate would violate the freeze.

**How to apply:** If beta review changes the qualitative safeguard interpretation, review the selected answer wording against the source document and update only the safeguard interpretation and focused tests. Do not silently tune frozen weights or thresholds from an individual fixture.
